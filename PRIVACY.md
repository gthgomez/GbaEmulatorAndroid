# Flowframe (GbaEmulatorAndroid) Privacy Policy

> **Play Store URL placeholder:** `TODO_PRIVACY_URL`

## Summary

Flowframe is a **local-only** GBA emulator shell. Your ROMs, saves, and save states stay on your device.

## Data collection

- **No account required.** The app does not create user accounts or collect personal identifiers.
- **No network access.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission and does not transmit your data to our servers.
- **No third-party analytics or ads** in the configuration described here.

## Permissions

- **Vibrate:** optional haptic feedback for on-screen controls only.

The app does **not** request accessibility services, overlay permissions, or broad storage access. ROM and save I/O uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) with user-selected files.

## Data storage

- ROM files are read from locations you choose via SAF; the app does not bundle copyrighted ROMs.
- Cartridge saves and emulator save states are stored in app-private storage and exported only when you explicitly choose a destination via SAF.

## Export and backup

Save and save-state export/import are **user-initiated** only — nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

## Erase data

Uninstalling the app removes app-private save data. Exported files you saved elsewhere remain under your control.
